# Design brief and checklist

Copy of the live brief (Claude Docs), as of 14 Nov 2026, plus the security changes made after it.

## Summary
The building is about 60 × 24.5 ft. The road side faces north-east and the back faces south-west.
Neighbours will build on the north-west and south-east walls.
- **Ground floor:** shops, a godown and a toilet.
- **1st floor:** shops, plus the home's living room, dining, dry and wet kitchen, and Bedroom 1 (for visiting parents).
- **2nd floor:** master bedroom, Bedroom 2, Bedroom 3, family lounge, study, utility and an open sit-out.
- **Terrace:** on top.

Everything is arranged around one courtyard, open from the 1st floor to the sky.

**Changes made to meet the checklist:** split wet and dry kitchen (the family stair moved to the back
to make room), an open entrance view to the courtyard, puja in the north-east corner of the living room,
the water tank in the south-west of the terrace, built-in storage throughout, laundry on the 2nd floor,
a lockable door between the shop landing and the home, and the family stair continuing to the terrace.

**Added after the brief (security):** glass faces only the inside courtyard. There are fixed steel grids
over the courtyard and the back court at roof level, and full-height grilles plus a grille roof on the
road deck and sit-out. The back court has full-height side walls. A safety grille door goes behind the
main door, and a steel door on the terrace. A 2nd-floor gallery now links the family stair to the lounge.

## Open layout
- Living, dining, the dry kitchen and the foyer are open to each other around the courtyard.
- **Entrance view:** from the foyer you see the courtyard tree. The family stair has open risers.
- Doors are 3'6"–4' for the main door and 3' for rooms. The Bedroom 1 bath door is 2'9" or wider, with no step.
- Glass sliders face the courtyard. Pocket or sliding doors go on the dressing rooms and the wet kitchen.
- The courtyard is the double-height feature. A double-height living room is optional, at the cost of 2nd-floor lounge space.
- Built-in wardrobes and lofts replace freestanding furniture.

## Water and energy
- **Water tank:** raised 6–8 ft on a stand, with a pressure pump for the 2nd floor. Whole-house sediment filter, plus RO in the dry kitchen.
- **Solar:** a solar water heater above the stacked bathrooms, and rooftop PV over the hall and Bedroom 3. Fit the panel anchors before waterproofing, and lay a spare conduit to the main board now.
- **Rainwater:** downpipes on the neighbour-wall sides. Harvesting goes to a filter and a recharge pit under the ground-floor corridor; check with JNAC whether it's mandatory.
- **Greywater:** keep bathroom and washing-machine drains separate from the toilet lines, for later reuse on the terrace garden.

## Electrical, smart home and safety
- **CCTV conduits:** street gate, corridor, stair landings, godown, shops and the home door. Video doorbell at the home door, plus a call button at the street.
- **Smart lighting:** neutral wire and deep boxes at every switchboard, and Wi-Fi access points on each floor.
- **Inverter and meters:** separate inverter circuits. The shops get their own meter.
- **AC:** concealed copper pipes in every bedroom and the living room. Outdoor units on the terrace or on brackets, never on neighbour walls.
- **Earthing:** 2+ earth pits, an RCCB on each board, surge protection and a lightning arrester.
- **Stairs:** handrails on both sides of the common stair, 6–7" risers and 10–11" treads, anti-skid edges, two-way switches. Railing gaps under 4" on the open-riser stair.
- **Fire and escape:** two ways out of the 1st floor. The terrace door opens from inside without a key. One openable grille per floor. Extinguishers in the kitchen and godown, smoke detectors on each floor.

## Green spaces
- A vertical garden in the ground-floor corridor and plants at the home door.
- A courtyard tree (champa or frangipani) in a deep planter.
- Indoor plants along the courtyard glass: snake plant, ZZ plant, money plant, areca palm.
- A private seat in the 1st-floor back court, the road-facing deck, the sit-out with its pergola, and terrace planters over the master bedroom.
- Low-maintenance plants: bougainvillea, tulsi, curry leaf, lemongrass, aloe, portulaca, succulents. Drip irrigation on a timer.
