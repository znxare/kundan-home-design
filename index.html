Courtyard in the middle

Starts at the first floor and opens to the terrace; glass blocks in its floor light the godown below. Every main room looks into it through glass, and its raised, louvred cap lets hot air out while keeping rain and pigeons off.

Back court and jaali

The south-west back court brings in the summer and monsoon breeze. A terracotta jaali turns the harsh evening sun into soft patterned light.

Secure without losing light

Every opening a burglar could reach has fixed steel: a grille over the courtyard and the back court at roof level, full-height grilles on the road-facing deck and sit-out, and full-height side walls at the back. Glass faces only the inside courtyard, behind those grilles.

Private home stair

The common stair stops at the first floor for the shops. The family has its own stair under a skylight, and the second-floor sit-out and deck catch the morning sun from the road side.
